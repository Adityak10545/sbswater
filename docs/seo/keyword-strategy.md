# Keyword strategy, content and off-page opportunities

Based on what the site itself says SBS Water does: design, in-house
manufacturing (Talawade, Pune), installation and commissioning of CPU, WTP,
RO, ETP, ZLD, STP, cooling tower blowdown and process condensate treatment
plants, plus equipment supply and revamping. Main buyers are sugar mills,
distilleries and biofuel/ethanol plants, plus pharma, textile, food and
municipal clients. Offices are in Pune and Nipani (Karnataka); the projects
page lists sites in Maharashtra, Karnataka and Gujarat.

### Overlap to watch
- **Home vs. Services:** both list every service. Keep the home page
  brand/sector-led ("for sugar, distillery and process industries") and the
  Services page as the full catalogue.
- **RO vs. WTP vs. ETP:** the RO page explains RO as the final stage in every
  plant. Keep it focused on RO technology and recovery, and link out to the
  WTP/ETP pages rather than repeating their process descriptions.
- **CPU and ZLD** have no dedicated page yet. "Condensate polishing unit" is
  a core SBS specialism (featured first on the home and services pages) and currently only
  ranks via the home and services pages.

Do **not** create city "service area" pages (e.g. "ETP in Kolhapur") unless
each has genuine projects and distinct content — thin location pages are
treated as doorway pages.

### Article ideas (for the Blogs section, once real)
Each should link to the matching service page.
- How a condensate polishing unit cuts fresh-water intake in a distillery → CPU page
- ETP design for seasonal sugar-mill effluent (pH 4–5, COD up to 10,000 ppm) → ETP page
- RO recovery: why 85–90% matters and how two-stage RO achieves it → RO page
- Meeting CPCB/MPCB ZLD norms for molasses-based distilleries → ZLD page
- Monsoon turbidity swings and WTP design for river/canal water → WTP page
- Revamp vs. rebuild: debottlenecking an existing treatment plant → Services

Have an engineer review every technical claim before publishing. Avoid
large volumes of generic AI-written posts.

### On-page items needing approval
- The home page shows animated counters starting at "00". Crawlers
  generally see the final numbers only if the JS runs; consider putting the
  real figures (120+ projects, 60+ clients, 18 ongoing) in the HTML.
- Projects page description says "53+ plants delivered" while the home and
  careers pages say "120+ projects". Confirm which figure applies where.
- `about.html` description previously mentioned DM plants; DM is not listed
  on the Services page. Confirm whether DM plants are offered.

## Off-page readiness

**No live backlink audit was possible** — it needs an external data source
(Search Console *Links* report after launch, or Ahrefs/Semrush/Moz).

Legitimate opportunities to pursue manually (nothing has been submitted):

- **Client and partner listings:** OEM/EPC partners named on the site (Praj,
  Thermax, ISGEC, KBK Chem) and clients may have vendor/partner pages — ask
  for a listing or case-study mention.
- **Industry associations:** ISMA (Indian Sugar & Bio-Energy Manufacturers
  Association), Grain Ethanol Manufacturers Association, Deccan Sugar
  Institute / VSI (Vasantdada Sugar Institute) events and vendor directories,
  Indian Water Works Association (IWWA), MCCIA (Pune chamber of commerce).
- **Trade publications:** sugar/ethanol and water-industry magazines
  (e.g. Indian Sugar, Chini Mandi, Water Today, EverythingAboutWater) —
  pitch technical articles or project stories, not paid links.
- **Business profiles (keep NAP identical to the site):** Google Business
  Profile, Bing Places, LinkedIn company page (already linked), IndiaMART /
  TradeIndia (if used for sales), Justdial.
- **Linkable assets:** a water-recovery / CPU sizing explainer, a published
  case-study with measured before/after data, or an annual
  "distillery water reuse" summary from SBS project data.

Avoid paid links, mass directory submissions, link exchanges and fake reviews.
