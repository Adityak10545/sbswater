About SBS Water

Sustainable Business Solutions for Water & Wastewater Treatment

Since 2019, SBS Water & Infra Projects Pvt. Ltd. has designed, manufactured and commissioned water and wastewater treatment plants for industries across India and abroad. From our manufacturing unit in Talawade, Pune, we deliver condensate polishing units, ETPs, STPs, RO and ZLD systems built around each client's water chemistry, capacity and reuse goals. Every plant is engineered for long service life, stable performance and maximum water recovery.

Complete treatment solutions, from raw water to zero discharge

Services.

01/
Condensate Polishing Units (CPU)

Treat distillery and excess sugar condensate into reusable process water, cutting fresh-water intake.

02/
Effluent Treatment Plants (ETP)

Physical, chemical, biological and tertiary treatment for compliant discharge or reuse of industrial effluent.

03/
Zero Liquid Discharge (ZLD)

Membrane, concentration and evaporation schemes that recover maximum water and eliminate liquid discharge.

04/
Sewage Treatment Plants (STP)

Biological and tertiary systems for domestic and commercial sewage, with treated water ready for reuse.

05/
Water Treatment Plants (WTP)

Raw and process water treatment for sugar and distillery plants, with filtration, UF and RO.

06/
Cooling Tower Blowdown Treatment

Recover and reuse CT blowdown and injection water by controlling dissolved and suspended solids.

07/
Pharma & Chemical Treatment (PCTP)

Treatment schemes for high-strength pharmaceutical and chemical process wastewater.

08/
Equipment Supply & Plant Revamping

Debottlenecking, upgrades and equipment replacement that lift performance without rebuilding the plant.
