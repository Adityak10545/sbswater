Who We Are

SBS Water & Infra Projects Pvt. Ltd.

SBS Water & Infra Projects Pvt. Ltd. is a Pune-based water and wastewater treatment company. We design, engineer, manufacture and commission treatment plants for industries across India and overseas. Since our incorporation in 2019, we have helped industries treat, recover and reuse water more efficiently, so wastewater becomes a resource instead of a disposal problem.

We build Condensate Polishing Units (CPU), Effluent Treatment Plants (ETP), Sewage Treatment Plants (STP), Water Treatment Plants (WTP), Reverse Osmosis (RO) and Demineralization (DM) systems, and Zero Liquid Discharge (ZLD) schemes, with deep experience in the sugar and distillery sectors.

Our team of 100+ engineers, technicians and project professionals works from our corporate office in Shivajinagar, Pune, our manufacturing unit at Talawade, Pune, and our regional office in Nipani, Karnataka. In-house manufacturing lets us control quality at every stage, from fabrication to commissioning.

Our Strengths

What Sets Us Apart

Every plant starts with a clear understanding of the client's water: its source, chemistry, flow and where the treated water will go.

High-Efficiency RO Designs

85–90% water recovery with less reject water.

Advanced Digester Technology

Up to 80–90% COD reduction.

Sustainable Water Reuse

Treated water goes back into use for boiler feed, fermentation and cooling towers.

Wide Technology Range

Biological (MBBR, SBR, ASP, CSTR, eSBR, EGBR), membrane (RO, UF, MBR), thermal & ZLD, and advanced oxidation (Ozonation, DAF, AOP).

In-House Manufacturing

Our Talawade facility gives us full control over quality, timelines and fabrication.

New Plants & Revamping

Solutions for greenfield installations as well as upgrades of existing plants.
