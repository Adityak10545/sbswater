Overview

What is a Water Treatment Plant?

Raw water from rivers, canals, dams and borewells contains suspended solids, turbidity, colour, iron, hardness and microorganisms. Used untreated, it causes scaling in heat exchangers and cooling towers, fouls equipment, disrupts fermentation and raises maintenance costs.

A Water Treatment Plant (WTP) removes these impurities through physical, chemical and disinfection stages, producing water that meets the quality required for each use. SBS Water & Infra Projects Pvt. Ltd. designs, manufactures, installs and commissions WTPs built around the client's source water, flow and end-use requirements.

  • Consistent water quality through every season
  • Protection against scaling and fouling
  • Water for process, cooling, utilities and drinking

Process

How our Water Treatment Plant works

Raw water passes through aeration, chemical treatment, clarification, multi-stage filtration, softening and disinfection. Stages are selected according to source water analysis.

  1. Raw Water Storage
  2. Cascade Aerator
  3. Flash Mixer (pH / PAC / Cl₂)
  4. Flocculation Tank
  5. HRSCC Clarifier
  6. Clear Water Tank
  7. Multi Grade Filter
  8. Activated Carbon Filter
  9. Softener Plant
  10. Bag Filter
  11. UV + Chlorine Dosing
  12. Treated Water Storage
  • Sludge withdrawn from the HRSCC
  • Physical, chemical & disinfection stages selected by source water analysis
  1. Step 01

    Raw Water Storage

    Raw water from the river, canal, dam or borewell is received in the raw water storage tank.

  2. Step 02

    Cascade Aerator

    Water cascades over steps, absorbing oxygen to oxidize iron and manganese and release odour-causing gases.

  3. Step 03

    Flash Mixer + Dosing

    pH correction chemicals, PAC and chlorine are dosed and rapidly mixed into the water.

  4. Step 04

    Flocculation

    Slow mixing allows fine particles to collide and form larger, heavier flocs.

  5. Step 05

    HRSCC

    The High Rate Solids Contact Clarifier settles flocs, removing most turbidity and suspended solids.

  6. Step 06

    Clear Water Tank

    Clarified water is collected here and pumped to the filtration stages.

  7. Step 07

    Multi Grade Filter

    Layers of graded media capture residual suspended solids and turbidity.

  8. Step 08

    Activated Carbon Filter

    Activated carbon removes colour, odour, organics and residual chlorine.

  9. Step 09

    Softener Plant

    Ion-exchange resin removes calcium and magnesium hardness to prevent scaling.

  10. Step 10

    Bag Filter

    Fine bag filtration catches remaining particles and resin fines.

  11. Step 11

    UV + Chlorine Dosing

    UV inactivates bacteria and pathogens; chlorine gives residual protection in storage.

  12. Step 12

    Treated Water Storage

    Treated water is stored and distributed for process, cooling, utility or drinking use.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

What is a Water Treatment Plant?

A Water Treatment Plant (WTP) treats raw water from rivers, canals, lakes or borewells to remove turbidity, suspended solids, hardness, colour, odour and microorganisms, making it suitable for process, utility or drinking use.

Which raw water sources can you treat?

We design WTPs for surface water (river, canal, dam, lake), groundwater (borewell) and recovered water such as polished condensate. The scheme is chosen after analysing the source water.

What is an HRSCC and why use it?

A High Rate Solids Contact Clarifier combines flocculation and settling in a compact unit. It handles high turbidity, especially during monsoon, with a smaller footprint than conventional clarifiers.

Can treated water be used for boilers?

WTP-treated and softened water suits cooling towers and general process use. For boiler feed, we add Reverse Osmosis and/or Demineralization (DM) stages to reach the required purity.

Can CPU-treated condensate be fed to the WTP?

Yes. Several of our projects combine a CPU with a WTP, so recovered condensate supplements raw water and reduces freshwater drawal.

Do you upgrade existing WTPs?

Yes. We troubleshoot, debottleneck and revamp existing WTPs, including filter media replacement, clarifier upgrades, capacity enhancement and adding softening or disinfection stages.
